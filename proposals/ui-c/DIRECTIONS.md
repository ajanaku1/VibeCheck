# VibeCheck UI-C directions

All three directions inherit the selected Neighborhood Patch brand: Hearth
Orange is the only accent, Oat is the communal field, Night Ink carries the
individual regions, and precise seams keep evidence types visibly separate.
They are isolated prototypes and do not import from or modify production UI.

## 1. Front Porch

- **Axis:** expansive, welcoming, landing-led.
- **Anchor:** one oversized orange neighborhood field physically gathers the
  recovery story and its three stages.
- **DFII:** impact 4 + fit 5 + feasibility 4 + performance 5 - consistency risk
  2 = **16**.
- **Wins when:** warmth and creator trust must be felt before operational detail.
- **Cost:** the overview shows fewer cases above the fold.

## 2. Common Room

- **Axis:** compact, spatial, workspace-led.
- **Anchor:** a persistent ink rail and one orange recovery room organize every
  protected view without becoming card soup.
- **DFII:** impact 4 + fit 4 + feasibility 4 + performance 5 - consistency risk
  2 = **15**.
- **Wins when:** the product should feel like a calm tool used every day.
- **Cost:** the denser shell is less emotionally expansive on first visit.

## 3. Living Record

- **Axis:** chronological, editorial, evidence-led.
- **Anchor:** a continuous orange seam carries remembered context, action, and
  outcome through every surface.
- **DFII:** impact 5 + fit 5 + feasibility 4 + performance 5 - consistency risk
  2 = **17**.
- **Wins when:** proving persistence and provenance is the strongest story.
- **Cost:** it feels more reflective than real-time.

## Required surfaces

Each direction includes interactive views for the public landing page, access
denied, recovery overview, and recovery case detail. Navigation and case rows
work; all protected content is absent from the denied view.
