# Render Route

Route: iterative local vector SVG
Decision: The Warm Overlap needs authored organic silhouettes while remaining distinct at 16px
Primary format: controlled 256×256 SVG construction and local browser rendering
Review output: exactly three primary candidates under `brand/review/`

Early direct-SVG rounds exposed repeated geometric drift into propellers, cards,
bars, paws, and flower-like patches. Those rounds were rejected and redrawn
locally. External raster exploration was considered but blocked before any
brief or image was transmitted, so no model output appears in the package. The
three review candidates vary composition and shape language—not color. Derived
assets were created only after Neighborhood Patch was selected.
