# VibeCheck Primary-Mark Selection

selected_candidate: candidate-c-neighborhood-patch.svg
selected_name: Neighborhood Patch
selected_by: project owner
selected_on: 2026-08-17

Neighborhood Patch was selected because its three unequal regions form one warm
communal field without drawing literal people, social-media symbols, or a
monitoring metaphor. It remains distinctive at favicon size and can extend into
a flexible visual system for cases, community moments, and motion.

Only this candidate may produce derived assets. Shared Ground and Common Table
remain review history and are not approved for production use.
