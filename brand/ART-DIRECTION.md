# VibeCheck Art Direction

Status: approved

Approved by: project owner
Approval recorded: 2026-08-17

## Concept — The Warm Overlap

Signature element: The Warm Overlap.

The new direction treats “vibe” as the atmosphere people create together. It is
not a signal to measure and not a fracture to diagram. Three distinct, soft
forms settle onto one compact, asymmetric shared ground. Each form keeps its own
outer edge, while the place supporting all three becomes the only Hearth Orange
region. The signature is the **warm overlap**: individual energies leaning into
the same welcoming base.

The transformation is:

`individual energy → shared presence → warmer community`

The obvious community logo—a heart, house, handshake, chat bubble, or people in
a circle—is rejected. Literal audio waves, equalizers, music notes, and pulse
lines are also rejected; they turn “vibe” into a music feature or analytics
signal. The mark must feel social and alive without illustrating social media.

## Mandatory visual ingredients

- **The warm overlap:** one elongated shared ground in Hearth Orange; never a
  central dot, badge, or pasted-on hub.
- **Three distinct forms:** soft, unequal, and visibly individual at their outer
  edges; they are abstract presences, not heads or bodies.
- **An asymmetric huddle:** close and energetic, with visibly different heights,
  widths, and lean; never a perfect radial badge or a row of chart bars.
- **One-color survival:** the overlap must still be understandable through
  interlocking shape and negative space when color is unavailable.

## Reference qualities, not reference assets

- From Airbnb: human warmth, soft confidence, and immediate welcome.
- From Spotify: bold reduction, cultural energy, and strong small-size recall.
- From TikTok: social immediacy, playful tension, and an internet-native rhythm.

Do not reuse their colors, silhouettes, typography, symbols, or visual effects.
In particular: no Bélo-like loop, no stacked broadcast arcs, no musical note,
and no cyan/pink offset shadow.

## Material language

Use bold, tactile poster-ink forms on an Oat field: flat color, soft but authored
curves, and edges that feel cut by hand with precision rather than generated
from perfect circles. Night Ink holds the individual forms; Hearth Orange
belongs only to the common ground. The mood is a warm neighborhood poster with
the graphic confidence of a modern social product.

There are no gradients, transparency effects, glass, glow, shadows, or faux 3D.
Warmth comes from color, proportion, closeness, and the central interaction—not
decoration.

## Composition

- Center one compact three-form huddle in a square field with generous space.
- Keep the arrangement subtly off-axis so it feels conversational, not
  institutional or diagrammatic.
- Keep all forms moving through a shared layered composition; never arrange
  three arms around a hub or point forms toward a center.
- Let the shared orange ground occupy roughly one-fifth of the mark; it should
  be discovered immediately but never read as a progress bar or underline.
- Build the orange region into the contact geometry beneath the forms; never
  paste a circle, dot, badge, or hub over the composition.
- Keep every outer form broad enough to remain distinct at favicon scale.
- Keep the primary mark free of text; any future wordmark sits beside it.

## Palette

- **Hearth Orange `#F2643C`:** mandatory and the only accent; used solely for
  the shared ground.
- **Night Ink `#181514`:** primary neutral for the individual forms and type.
- **Oat `#FFF3E2`:** warm field and negative space.
- **Warm Ash `#786A62`:** supporting neutral for interface use, not a second
  accent in the primary mark.

## Dual-legibility target

At 16px, the mark reads as a warm, compact gathering on one shared ground. At
512px, the viewer discovers three non-identical forms and the precise common
base that turns separate energy into community.

## Forbidden list

Automatically reject any candidate containing or resembling:

- Airbnb's Bélo, Spotify's wave stack, TikTok's musical note, or any other
  recognizable brand asset;
- cyan/pink offset shadows, Spotify green, Airbnb coral, or a copied reference
  palette;
- people in a circle, heads-and-shoulders silhouettes, handshakes, linked hands,
  hearts, houses, chat bubbles, or location pins;
- audio waves, broadcast arcs, equalizer bars, music notes, play buttons, pulse
  lines, gauges, charts, bar charts, progress bars, or checkmarks;
- eyes, lenses, radar, crosshairs, shields, warning marks, bells, or surveillance
  and moderation imagery;
- perfect radial symmetry, camera apertures, recycling loops, Venn diagrams,
  flower petals, fidget-spinners, propellers, three-arm clusters, central dots
  with spokes, or generic network nodes;
- literal suns, flames, stars, sparkles, glowing orbs, magic dust, robot heads,
  brains, circuits, hexagons, or cubes;
- letters, numbers, wordmarks, watermarks, rainbows, extra accent hues,
  gradients, gloss, glass, shadows, textures, or busy scenes.

## Approval gate

The direction was approved in words before any logo candidate was rendered.
Hearth Orange and The Warm Overlap are mandatory for all three primary-mark
explorations; no derived asset may be created before one primary mark is
selected.
