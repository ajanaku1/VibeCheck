# VibeCheck Brand Truth

- **name:** VibeCheck
- **purpose:** Help creators notice community strain and guide people back toward a shared sense of belonging.
- **core_verb:** Gather.
- **metaphor:** Three distinct regions form one warm neighborhood patch; individuality stays visible while the shared field makes them feel like a community.
- **palette:** One mandatory accent—Hearth Orange `#F2643C`—with Night Ink `#181514`, Oat `#FFF3E2`, and Warm Ash `#786A62` neutrals.
- **constraints:** The primary mark must work on the web, as a Telegram avatar, and at 16px; preserve a compact communal silhouette in one color; use the shared-overlap motif; and never copy Airbnb's Bélo, Spotify's wave stack, TikTok's note or color-offset effect, or generic community and moderation symbols.
- **selected_signature:** Neighborhood Patch—one softly irregular Hearth Orange field holding three unequal Night Ink regions separated by precise Oat seams.
- **material_language:** Native flat vector with tactile poster-ink massing, authored soft corners, no effects, and no simulated depth.
- **size_floor:** 16px for the full-color mark; internal seam detail becomes fully legible at 32px.
- **forbidden:** Literal people, hearts, houses, hands, chat bubbles, music/audio marks, checks, charts, surveillance/moderation symbols, copied reference-brand assets, radial clichés, extra accent hues, gradients, shadows, glass, or decorative texture.
